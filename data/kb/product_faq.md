# Loopwise Product FAQ

Loopwise is a project-tracking tool for small teams (boards, tasks, sprints, time tracking).

## Q: How do I invite a teammate?
A: Go to Settings > Members > Invite, enter their email, and choose a role (Viewer, Editor, Admin). They'll receive an email invite that expires after 7 days.

## Q: How do I move a task between boards?
A: Open the task, click the board name at the top, and select the destination board from the dropdown. Task history and comments move with it.

## Q: Can I export my data?
A: Yes. Go to Settings > Export, choose CSV or JSON, and select a date range. Exports are emailed as a download link within 10 minutes.

## Q: How do I change my workspace name?
A: Only workspace Admins can do this, under Settings > Workspace > General.

## Q: Does Loopwise have a mobile app?
A: Yes, iOS and Android apps are available. Feature parity is about 80% of the web app — bulk task editing and custom fields are web-only as of this doc.

## Q: How do time tracking entries get approved?
A: A team lead marks entries as "Approved" under the Time tab. Approved entries lock and cannot be edited by the original submitter.
