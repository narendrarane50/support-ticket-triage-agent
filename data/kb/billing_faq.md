# Loopwise Billing FAQ

## Plans
- Starter: $9/user/month, billed monthly. Up to 10 members.
- Team: $15/user/month, billed monthly, or $12/user/month billed annually. Unlimited members.
- Enterprise: custom pricing, contact sales.

## Q: When am I charged?
A: On the day you upgrade, then monthly (or annually) on that same calendar day. If a member is added mid-cycle, the charge is prorated for the remainder of the current cycle.

## Q: How do I update my card on file?
A: Settings > Billing > Payment Method. Failed payments retry automatically 3 times over 7 days before the workspace is downgraded to a read-only state.

## Q: How do I get an invoice/receipt?
A: Settings > Billing > Invoices. All past invoices are downloadable as PDF.

## Q: I was charged twice this month, what happened?
A: This can happen if a member count crosses a plan threshold mid-cycle, triggering a prorated charge in addition to the regular renewal charge. Check Settings > Billing > Invoices — each charge should correspond to a separate line item. If the two charges are for the exact same amount on the exact same day, that is a billing error and should be escalated, not resolved by the agent.

## Q: Do you offer discounts?
A: Annual billing gives a 20% discount versus monthly. Loopwise does not offer ad-hoc discretionary discounts outside of that; any such request must be escalated to a human.
