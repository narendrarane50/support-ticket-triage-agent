# Loopwise Refund Policy (internal reference — support agents may quote this verbatim)

1. Full refunds are available if requested within **14 calendar days** of the charge date, for any reason.
2. Refund requests made **after 14 calendar days** of the charge date are **not eligible** for a standard refund. These may only be approved as a goodwill exception by a support **manager**, never by a first-line agent or an automated system.
3. Annual plans: the 14-day window applies to the annual charge date, not the original signup date. There are no partial/prorated refunds for unused months of an annual plan outside the 14-day window.
4. Refunds are never issued for usage-based overage charges (e.g., storage overage), regardless of timing.
5. Any refund request that includes an accusation of fraud, an unauthorized charge, or a threat of chargeback/legal action must be escalated to a human immediately, regardless of the 14-day window.
6. A support agent (human or automated) may confirm the policy and check the charge date, but must never promise a refund amount or approve a refund outright. Refunds are only executed by a manager through the billing system.
