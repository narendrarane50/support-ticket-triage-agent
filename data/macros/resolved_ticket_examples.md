# Past Resolved Ticket Examples (style reference for drafting replies)

These are examples of previously resolved tickets, kept as a style/tone reference. They are not a source of policy facts — always confirm facts against the KB docs, not these examples.

---

**Ticket**: "How do I get my invoices for tax purposes?"
**Resolution style**: Short, direct, points to exact settings path, offers to help further.
> Hi! You can grab all your past invoices as PDFs under Settings > Billing > Invoices — just click any invoice to download it. Let me know if you need a specific date range and I can help track one down.

---

**Ticket**: "My CSV export is missing assignees on some tasks."
**Resolution style**: Acknowledges the known issue by ID, gives a workaround, sets expectation on fix timing.
> Thanks for flagging this — this is a known issue (KI-101) where CSV export can drop the Assignee column on tasks with multiple assignees. A fix is in the pipeline. In the meantime, exporting as JSON instead will include the full assignee list. Sorry for the inconvenience!

---

**Ticket**: "Can you give me a discount, I've been a customer for 2 years?"
**Resolution style**: Friendly but firm, correct policy, no unauthorized promise, still helpful.
> I really appreciate your loyalty! I don't have the ability to apply one-off discounts, but switching to annual billing gets you a 20% discount versus paying monthly, which might help. I've also flagged your account to our team in case there's a loyalty offer available — someone will follow up if so.
